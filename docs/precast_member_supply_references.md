# Precast-member supply references

Living project, updated continuously.

Reviewed 16 November 2026. HS Hwasung's PC-business project page names **Samsung Electronics Pyeongtaek semiconductor factory**, at Yeoyeom-ri, Godeok-myeon, Pyeongtaek-si. It lists **PC COLUMN, BEAM fabrication and delivery**, with a company-reported work period of **1 October 2015 to 1 March 2016**. [Official project page](https://www.hwasung.com/index.php/p/business/pc/?act=view&category=19&depart=7&hcode=002.008&id=392), project entry 392.

The reference supports a precast-concrete column/beam supply role. It does not supply member dimensions, concrete strength, reinforcement, prestressing, quantities, connection details, erection methods or installed positions. Fabrication and delivery are not independently verified installation or completion. The stated period is retained as the company's work-period label, not a campus commissioning date.

The early date alone is insufficient to assign the work to P1 or any individual building. Generic descriptions of double-tee slabs or other all-PC systems elsewhere on the company's website are not applied to this project. The company name Hwasung does not make this a Samsung Hwaseong-campus reference: this particular page explicitly names Pyeongtaek.

M33 in the [material catalog](construction_materials_sources.json) records this additional named supply reference. It is kept separate from the existing GPC Green-dong references, IS Dongseo's 154 kV project labels and Samil C&S's P5 FAB2 contract. Similar material families do not establish a shared contract, member schedule or building identity. No model element or installed material property is changed.

## Korea Ocean Platform


Korea Ocean Platform's company-hosted [January 2026 brochure](https://m.kop.co.kr/files/pdf/kop_brochure.pdf) identifies **Samsung Electronics Pyeongtaek P-Project** and **PC-member fabrication and delivery** on PDF page 13, printed page 25. The brochure states that the company adopted the Korea Ocean Platform name from Hanseong PC Construction. It is not identified with HS Hwasung, GPC or another concrete supplier by name similarity.

The caption is listed on the brochure's commercial-facilities portfolio page; that heading does not establish the building's use. Nearby references to Pyeongtaek STV and other Pyeongtaek logistics centers are not combined with Samsung's reference. The caption supplies no fab number, member schedule, quantities, delivery period, installed positions or acceptance results. It is kept as an additional supplier reference without mapping its photographs to a model box or floor.

The brochure separately presents generic semiconductor-facility PC product families and concrete-development capabilities. Neither those families nor the hollow-core-slab appendix's dimensions, strengths, topping or load tables are established as installed Pyeongtaek specifications. Photographs and technical drawings are not reproduced.


SUP-PC02 in the [supplemental index](supplemental_supplier_sources.json) records this additional company reference. It is separate from HS Hwasung's M33 material row.
