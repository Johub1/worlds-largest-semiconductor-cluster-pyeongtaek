Living project, updated continuously.

Samyoung's company profile lists P3 phase-specific electrical, temporary-power, communications and control work. It also names P4 Ph1 Complex-building (복합동) general-control and general-electrical work. The P3L Ph2 reference retains the source labels ‘5F FSF, south SUP, north SUP’; these are not translated into modeled rooms or coordinates. [Company profile](https://www.samyoungco.com/file/(주)삼영기업%20지명원%20국문.pdf), PDF page 14, printed pages 25–26.

Separate references identify fire-electrical work for P3 Ph1 Green-dong, 154kV and Complex buildings, P3 Ph2 Complex/Green-dong, and P4 Ph1 Green-dong. Fire-electrical work remains distinct from fire-mechanical contracts. The 154kV label does not establish the voltage of a fire-system circuit.

PDF page 20 lists P3 wastewater-treatment and ‘P4 PJT stage-3 Godeok public wastewater-treatment’ fire-electrical work. The stage label does not establish additional treatment capacity, facility ownership or a match to an internal Green-dong.

The source's ordering-party column names 삼성중공업 for the selected P3L electrical references, 삼성ENG for the selected P3/P4 Complex-building control/electrical references, and 삼성전자 for the selected fire-electrical references. These labels do not reconstruct the full legal contracting chain. Listed work periods are supplier reference periods, not campus commissioning dates. Unmatched client/project rows are excluded from model mapping. No cable size, equipment count, wiring route, floor height or installed fire-system specification is assigned.
