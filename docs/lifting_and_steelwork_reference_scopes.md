# Pipe-module lifting and P5 steelwork references

Living project, updated continuously.

[Freyssinet Korea](https://www.freyssinet.co.kr/en/) identifies a pipe-module project named Samsung-Pyungtak P4_Ph1 and describes six 40-ton strand jacks. This is a supplier's construction reference. The ratings do not establish the module weight or permitted combined lifting load. The jacks are construction equipment, not six permanent campus machines. Lifting height, position, date and pipe contents are not established.

[Shinhan SNG's steelwork references](https://www.shsng.com/ko/performance_steel?no=1103&sCate=3) list separate Pyeongtaek P5 FAB1 lot 6 and FAB2 lot 3 packages under Samsung C&T. Their scale fields state eleven above-ground floors and 21,737 t and 14,402 t respectively. Another row names P5 Ph1 UPW pipe-rack installation under Samsung E&A, with three above-ground floors and 982 t; that row does not directly name Pyeongtaek, so site matching remains open.

These are separate supplier reference fields, not verified campus totals or accepted delivery quantities. They are not added together. Building-floor labels do not identify cleanroom levels, and pipe-rack levels do not identify fab production floors. No modeled floor count, height, coordinates, installed material grade, contracting total or project identity is changed. Dates and current completion status remain unverified.
