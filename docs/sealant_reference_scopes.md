# Sealant references: named warehouse work and scope limits

Living project, updated continuously.

Reviewed 16 November 2026. KCC Silicone's manufacturer-hosted approval-material booklet includes a delivery/reference table for **Samsung Electronics Pyeongtaek factory logistics warehouse new construction**. The row lists Pyeongtaek-si as the location, Samsung C&T in the **contractor** column, **glass glazing** as the application, and **SL907 Premium** (`SL907 프리미엄`) as the product. [Manufacturer booklet](https://www.kccsilicone.com/upload/KCCSILICONE_Approval_Sealant_kor_2024.pdf), PDF page 38, printed page 74.

This is a named manufacturer reference for a glazing-sealant product. The table gives no street address, application date, joint dimensions, installed quantity or individual acceptance result. The booklet's approval-material title is not proof that a particular project approval or inspection was completed.

The row does not establish structural silicone glazing, cleanroom compatibility, chemical resistance, a fire-resistance duration or campus-wide use. The product is not assigned to fab interiors or to every glazed facade. The exact product designation is retained without importing specifications from a current or different product sheet.

## Nearby names kept separate

The same booklet lists **Samsung Electronics Pyeongtaek dormitory** with KCC Construction, fire-zone joint sealing and **SW9535A** (PDF page 40, printed page 79). This is a separate dormitory reference; it does not locate the building inside the modeled campus or establish the complete wall/joint assembly's fire rating. It is not added as a fab material record.

Another row names **Godeok Samsung Techno Valley**, Yojin Construction and SL819 for structural silicone glazing (PDF page 35, printed page 68). Similarity of project names does not identify this as Samsung Electronics' semiconductor campus. SL819 and that structural-glazing application are not transferred to the warehouse row.

## Use alongside the model

M32 in the [material catalog](construction_materials_sources.json) records the warehouse reference. The modeled `LOG-WH` box is a separately sourced warehouse candidate; identity with the booklet's warehouse is unconfirmed. No element receives a product assignment, new geometry, property, quantity or confidence grade from this reference. The table itself and its project images are not reproduced.
