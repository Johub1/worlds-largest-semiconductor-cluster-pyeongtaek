# Stainless tube reference scopes

Living project, updated continuously. Evidence checked on 15 November 2026.

Sung Kwang Metal's brochure contains two relevant reference entries. Row 65 names Pyeongtaek Godeok P3/P4 and lists KS D 3576 and KS D 3536, with a contract quantity of 55 TON. Row 69 names a semiconductor cleanroom in Pyeongtaek Godeok and lists KS D 3536 with 400TON. The Client labels are ‘삼성반도체’ and ‘삼성전자’, respectively. [Manufacturer catalog, available from its homepage](https://www.sksts.co.kr/), PDF page 34, printed pages 66–67.

The official Korean standards register identifies [KS D 3576](https://www.standard.go.kr/KSCI/standardIntro/getStandardSearchView.do?ksNo=KSD3576&menuId=919&reformNo=&tmprKsNo=KSD3576&topMenuId=502&upperMenuId=503) as stainless steel pipes for piping and [KS D 3536](https://www.standard.go.kr/KSCI/standardIntro/getStandardSearchView.do?ksNo=KSD3536&menuId=919&reformNo=13&tmprKsNo=KSD3536&topMenuId=502&upperMenuId=503) as stainless steel tubes for machine structural purposes. These titles do not identify the actual service or installed location of either reference entry.

The quantities are supplier-listed contract quantities. They are not allocated between fabs, treated as accepted installations, or added together. The Client labels do not establish the legal purchaser or contract chain. Contract independence, delivery dates, pipe sizes, stainless grades and surface finishes remain unverified. The second entry has no fab number. The structural-tube entry does not establish UPW or process-gas piping.

The brochure's general manufacturing diagram on PDF page 6 covers forming and welding, solution heat treatment, pickling, eddy-current testing and hydrostatic testing. This is a factory process description, not proof of tests performed on these particular deliveries. It does not specify field hook-up welding procedures.

No model geometry, installed material property or confidence grade is changed by this note.
