# System-ceiling components and modular installation

Living project, updated continuously.

Shinsung E&G's current [company brochure](https://www.shinsungeng.com/m15.php), file labelled 260623, PDF page 13 (printed 24–25), describes ceiling supports, a grid, fan-filter units, lighting and infill panels. These are general product roles; they do not identify installed materials, dimensions or quantities at Pyeongtaek.

A paper by the manufacturer's researchers in the [2023 KSME plant conference proceedings](https://ksme.or.kr/UploadData/Editor/Conference/202308/BA7C49B85D8D4E298AE6DC2B0FE7E22D.pdf), PDF pages 199–200, paper 23PT-Fr01B099, describes assembly at floor level followed by lifting, support adjustment, connections and finishing. It reports a 5.2 × 7.5 m module and an unnamed domestic semiconductor application in 2022. That example does not identify Pyeongtaek. Its reported 66% reduction concerns overhead work, not total construction time or accident frequency.

The current brochure's HPL-050, HPL-060 and HPL-080 specifications are kept separate from the 2023 research equipment. Lifting height is not building floor height. The brochure separately captions a 6.5 × 3.5 m working area and lists the HPL-080 vehicle length and width as 6,500 mm and 3,500 mm. These labels are not treated as verified platform or ceiling-module dimensions. Neither source assigns a lift model, ceiling grid, structural load or installation route to a modeled fab. A different supplier's Pyeongtaek reference cannot establish use of Shinsung's equipment. No geometry, installed material properties or equipment counts are changed.
