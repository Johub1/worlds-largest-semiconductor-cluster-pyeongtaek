# Utility hook-up and supplier fabrication

Working evidence note, 15 November 2026. Living project, updated continuously.

Wooshin Engineering (우신엔지니어링) names utility hook-up work at Pyeongtaek P1L, P3L and P4L. Its homepage calls P1L integrated secondary piping; its business page groups the named lines under FT Hook-up. These labels are preserved without equating their contract scopes. The company describes factory spool fabrication for gas, ultrapure water, cooling water, exhaust and vacuum services, followed by site delivery and equipment connections. The references do not identify installed pipe grades, dimensions, weld procedures or equipment positions. Company-wide machinery and general manufacturing capabilities are not site specifications. [Business scope](https://www.wooshineng.kr/business.html), [homepage references](https://www.wooshineng.kr/).

Hana B&T (하나비앤티)'s selected 2019–2024 references name P4 PH3 fire-extinguishing-gas piping, a Green-dong building's secondary-side gas piping, and valve-room modules and primary-side piping for the building labelled 기술2동. “Technology Building 2” is a descriptive translation. P2 references name fire-system pipes/supports and alarm-valve skids. The client and EPC/end-user columns are retained without reconstructing the full contracting chain. Steel tonnage and pipe-spool DI are separate measures, not additive campus quantities. The Green-dong entry does not identify a fab. [Supplier references](https://hanabnt.com/sub/sub01_04.php).

The same table separately lists a UT-building catwalk without a P-number or fire-system designation. It is not assigned to P2 or combined with the fire-system scopes. No installed grade, joint detail, seismic rating or quantity has been added to the model.
