# Raised floors, equipment bases and vibration assessment

Working evidence note, 15 November 2026. Living project, updated continuously.

SB TECH names access-floor work for a Pyeongtaek P-Project and describes participation from the early stages of the Godeok complex. Its separate K2/3 project labels are not converted to model P2/P3. The page distinguishes access-floor construction from equipment-base work without assigning an individual base to a Pyeongtaek tool. [Company references](https://www.sb-tech.co.kr/kor/business/business.php?part=1).

Its general catalog distinguishes aluminum blind, perforated and grated panels, illustrating surface and airflow functions. Catalog dimensions, opening ratios and load ratings do not identify installed Pyeongtaek products. The catalog's mixed kgf/lb entries are not combined. [Panel families](https://www.sb-tech.co.kr/kor/product/duct.php). The supplier separately describes [understructures](https://www.sb-tech.co.kr/kor/product/psp.php) and customer-selected [equipment-base types](https://www.sb-tech.co.kr/kor/product/module.php). These remain distinct from structural floors and suspended-ceiling supports.

MDS Solution (엠디에스솔루션)'s 2019 reference table names Pyeongtaek lattice-beam and equipment-base (제진대) vibration-impact assessment and vibration measurement of equipment bases (제진대) at P1Line FAB, with 에스비테크 and KLA-Tencor respectively in its company-name column. This records measurement services, without test results, acceptance criteria, vibration grades or isolation-product specifications. The table year is not an individual measurement date. It supplies no lithography-tool model or installed count. [MDS references](https://www.mds-s.co.kr/page/page73).

Matching the name 에스비테크 to SB TECH does not establish corporate identity, a shared contract or an installed product. No model geometry, material property or confidence grade has changed.
