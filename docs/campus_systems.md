# Historical campus communications infrastructure

Working evidence note, 15 November 2026. Living project, updated continuously.

Samsung's two-page [Pyeongtaek WLAN case study](https://images.samsung.com/is/content/samsung/p5/sec/business/network/publication/case-study/CaseStudy_Samsung-Electronics-Pyeongtaek-Campus.pdf) describes wireless networking and fixed-mobile voice integration across offices, production areas and connecting zones. It reports redundant infrastructure, wireless intrusion prevention and central management. These are the publisher's implementation claims, not independently measured availability or a current network inventory.

The component list names an AP controller, access points, WIPS sensors/server, WEM, SCMExpress and OS7500. It supplies no device counts or placement coordinates, and does not map every listed product to an individual building or function. The text also describes outdoor-type access points in production areas with dust, rain and explosion-protection certifications; no certificate or installed variant is provided here.

The document describes one completed line and further construction. Its file metadata records August2018, which is not a commissioning date. The case is retained as a historical snapshot and is not expanded to today's P1–P4 campus. No equipment position, cable route, radio coverage or network segmentation is generated from it. Source photographs are not reproduced.
