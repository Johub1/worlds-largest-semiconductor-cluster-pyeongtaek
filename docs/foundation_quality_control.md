# Pile-foundation quality-control references

Working evidence note, 15 November 2026. Living project, updated continuously.

Piletech's project table names pile quality control and dynamic load-testing services for four Pyeongtaek FAB construction projects:

| Source project label | Listed service period | Client/contractor column |
|---|---|---|
| FAB 2-gi | January–November 2018 | Samsung C&T |
| FAB 3-gi | June 2020–May 2021 | Samsung C&T |
| FAB 4-gi | November 2021–June 2023 | Samsung C&T |
| FAB 5-gi | September 2023–June 2024 | Samsung Electronics / Samsung C&T |

These are supplier service periods, not complete construction schedules or acceptance dates. The printed labels do not independently map to model elements. The FAB 2-gi remark, “현장타설 PHC”, is retained without resolving its detailed pile type or extending it to other fabs. [Official references](https://piletech.co.kr/sub/trade.php).

The firm's general description distinguishes measurements during initial driving from later restrike testing. This explains a quality-control method without establishing the sequence used at an individual fab. Other test methods described on the website are not assigned to these four projects. [General test methods](https://piletech.co.kr/sub/business.php).

No test report, acceptance result, bearing resistance, settlement, pile length, diameter, count or layout was obtained. This supports a foundation quality-control note, with no pile geometry or material schedule added to the model. General PHC product descriptions remain separate.
