# Reinforcement reference scopes

Living project, updated continuously. Evidence checked on 15 November 2026.

## KCMT infrastructure reinforcement

KCMT's 2022 English brochure lists RS Ø25mm for slurry-wall reinforcement under a Pyeongtaek–Godeok infrastructure project naming Samsung Electronics. A separate row labelled Pyeongtaek P3 PROJECT lists VRS Ø25mm for tunnelling. [Manufacturer brochure](https://kcmt.co.kr/img/03/2022KCMT_brochure_EN.pdf), PDF page 21.

The company's delivery table lists 25mm for these project labels, with delivery-month entries beginning October 2020 and December 2021 respectively. It does not repeat the RS/VRS codes or give an end month. Open-ended dates establish neither continuing delivery today nor construction completion. [Official references](https://www.kcmt.co.kr/html/03_pr/04.php), rows 101 and 95.

These are manufacturer reference labels within its GFRP product context. They do not establish installed quantities, wall locations, reinforcement spacing or a fab-wide specification. The P3 row does not independently establish the model-building identity. Later recycled-PET/resin developments and catalog performance claims are not assigned to the earlier deliveries.

## Baro reinforcement supports and foundation reinforcement

Baro Construction Key-technology lists ‘삼성_반도체평택P3’ under Barona safety bar-chair references and ‘삼성_평택P5’ under Barona foundation-reinforcement references. These are separate product-reference lists. [Manufacturer brochure](https://www.baro-ck.com/upload/bbs/%EB%B0%94%EB%A1%9C%EA%B1%B4%EC%84%A4%EA%B8%B0%EC%88%A0_%EB%B8%8C%EB%A1%9C%EC%85%94_202411_%EC%B5%9C%EC%A2%85_%EA%B5%AD%EB%AC%B8.pdf), PDF page 8, printed pages 14–15.

The general bar-chair description concerns adjustable supports for upper reinforcement. Its 1.5–4 m range is catalog information, not a measured height at P3. The foundation-reinforcement page illustrates several product forms and thickness-saving claims; it does not identify the form used at P5 or an actual saving there. The page does not establish that both product families are temporary works removed after construction.

The abbreviated ‘삼성’ labels do not establish the legal purchaser or contract chain. No quantity, installation position, delivery date, accepted load or foundation thickness is assigned. Photographs without site labels are not treated as Pyeongtaek images. Other Pyeongtaek business-block references elsewhere in the brochure are not mapped to the semiconductor campus.

No model geometry, installed material property or confidence grade is changed by these notes.
