## Building records



- **PT-A001 — Unresolved aggregation difference.** In the September 2026 permit data for Godeok-dong 1696, the main permit's basic summary lists 19 buildings, while 20 building rows carry the same permit key. The rows exceed the summary by 42,285.72 m² of building area and 161,032.54 m² of gross floor area, matching one parking row. However, their floor-area-ratio area difference is 12,868.96 m², compared with 13,833.63 m² for that row; removing it leaves the rows 964.67 m² below the summary. The cause is not established. [Official permit service](https://www.hub.go.kr/portal/opn/tyb/idx-acpmslg.do).

- **PT-A002 — Unresolved building-area difference.** For the main permit at Godeok-dong 1695, the September 2026 data's 70 building rows total 935,651.56 m² of building area against 935,346.60 m² in the basic summary, a 304.96 m² difference. Gross floor area and floor-area-ratio area agree. No single row has a building area of 304.96 m²; the aggregation rule remains unconfirmed. [Official permit service](https://www.hub.go.kr/portal/opn/tyb/idx-acpmslg.do).

- **PT-A003 — Partly explained site-area scope.** The 1696 main-permit summary's 937,899.7 m² equals the sum of A1-1 804,237.8 m², A1-2 109,835.0 m² and C2-1 23,826.9 m² before the amendment in Gyeonggi Province Notice No.2026-189 (pp.25–26). C2-1 is a parking block overlapping a park. The lot's building-register title row shows 804,237.8 m², while amended A1-1 is 936,476.1 m² and A1-2 is 0. These figures are not interchangeable; their scope and update relationship remains unreconciled. The arithmetic does not establish the parking building's location or the cause of PT-A001. [Permit summary service](https://www.hub.go.kr/portal/opn/tyb/idx-acpmslg.do), [title-register service](https://www.hub.go.kr/portal/opn/tyb/idx-bdrg-ttlldr.do), [notice and attachments](https://www.eum.go.kr/web/gs/gv/gvGosiDet.jsp?seq=639228) (30 July 2026, pp.25–26).

- **PT-A004 — Floor-area inclusion remains unresolved.** In the two main permits, three buildings' floor-area sums exceed their respective gross floor areas by amounts matching rooftop-floor rows: 181.32 and 75.33 m² at lot 1695, and 177.19 m² at lot 1696. This statement is limited to those permits. It does not mean every difference in other permits on these lots is explained by rooftop floors. The legal inclusion basis remains unconfirmed, and rooftop areas cannot all be treated identically. [Official floor-record service](https://www.hub.go.kr/portal/opn/tyb/idx-acpmslg-flrcb.do).

- **PT-A005 — Small unresolved register difference.** For Godeok-dong 1730, the September 2026 data's nine individual building registers total 48,558.01 m² of gross floor area against 48,546.62 m² in the aggregate register. Floor-area-ratio area also differs by +11.39 m², while building area differs by -0.01 m². Equal displayed generation dates do not explain the differences or provide a complete revision history. [Official aggregate-register service](https://www.hub.go.kr/portal/opn/tyb/idx-bdrg-totldr.do).

## Financial and contract records



- **PT-F001 — Our aggregation corrected; tax assumption retained.** Of the 14 P4 construction contracts disclosed by Samsung C&T, Samsung E&A and Samsung Heavy Industries that we reviewed, 13 state amounts including VAT and one states an amount excluding VAT (item 9). Their displayed amounts cannot be added on a uniform basis. Normalisation assumes every contract is fully taxable at 10%; contract overlap and later amendments remain separate questions. This corrects our own aggregation. [VAT-exclusive filing](https://dart.fss.or.kr/dsaf001/main.do?rcpNo=20260831800587); [VAT-inclusive example](https://dart.fss.or.kr/dsaf001/main.do?rcpNo=20251212801338).

- **PT-F002 — Contract-balance movements largely account for the difference; residuals unresolved.** In the separate notes for 2021–2025, Samsung E&A's sales to Samsung Electronics differ from the buyer's purchases plus non-current-asset purchases by between KRW -1,504.5 billion and +663.8 billion a year. Movements in unbilled and over-billed construction balances with the same counterparty account for most of the difference in our calculation, leaving absolute residuals of approximately KRW 0.3–1.4 billion annually. This is our arithmetic reconciliation, not a company-published reconciliation or proof of the cause of each residual. The amounts are company-wide, not Pyeongtaek-only spending or cash payments. The source index below includes both counterparties' notes.

- **PT-F003 — Small counterparty residuals unresolved.** In the separate notes for 2021–2025, Samsung C&T's sales and other income from Samsung Electronics exceed the buyer's purchases plus non-current-asset purchases by KRW 0.001–3.4 billion a year, against annual amounts of approximately KRW 3.4–7.1 trillion. Account scope and recognition timing have not been reconciled line by line. The residual is not assigned to any Pyeongtaek contract. Both counterparties' notes appear in the source index below.

## Infrastructure and model interpretation



- **PT-I001 — Wastewater plan scopes unresolved.** The Han River Basin Environmental Office's consolidated wastewater-treatment basic-plan notice No. 2026-15 (item 36, PDF p.45) lists Godeok at 340,000 m³/day in four stages of 120,000, 67,500, 92,500 and 60,000 m³/day. Gyeonggi Province notice No. 2026-189 (30 July 2026, p.12, supporting facilities) gives 450,000 m³/day. Their stage, scope and approval relationship remain unreconciled in the reviewed sources. Neither figure is treated as current operating capacity; the 110,000 m³/day difference is not an inferred additional stage. The 13 January 2026 date of the consolidated notice concerns an amendment for another industrial complex, not a demonstrated Godeok amendment date. [Basic-plan notice](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulLsInfoP.do?admRulSeq=2100000272784), [attachment](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/flDownload.do?flSeq=163494657); [industrial-complex notice and attachments](https://www.eum.go.kr/web/gs/gv/gvGosiDet.jsp?seq=639228). The industrial-complex support schedule explicitly changed the wastewater treatment facility (then called a wastewater terminal treatment facility) from 345,000 to 450,000 m³/day in Notice 2023-181 of 30 June 2023 (PDF page 11, item 8). The preceding Notice 2022-168 lists 345,000; later notices 2024-1 and 2025-121 retain 450,000. The 2023 notice plans 445,939 m³/day of wastewater generation to be treated together: 4,503 domestic sewage, 435,711 from inside the industrial complex and 5,725 from the external Pyeongtaek Eco Center (PDF page 10, item 18). Within the notices reviewed, this places the documented 450,000 planning figure in June 2023. The earlier 345,000 figure also differs from the basic plan's 340,000; scope, stages and legal approval linkage remain unconfirmed. No treatment-plant count, fab allocation or current operating capacity is inferred. [Notice 2023-181](https://www.eum.go.kr/web/gs/gv/gvGosiDet.jsp?seq=570591), [Notice 2022-168](https://www.eum.go.kr/web/gs/gv/gvGosiDet.jsp?seq=531478).
