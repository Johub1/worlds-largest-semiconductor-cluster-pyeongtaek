# Supplier history and general lining processes

Living project, updated continuously.

Samil Flontech's company-history page records lower and upper P2 duct-segment supply to Samsung Electronics under 2020. It separately lists supply for Samsung Electronics' high-tech P3 PH2/PH3 projects under 2022, P3 PH4 and P4 PH1/PH2 projects under 2023, and a P4 PH3 project under 2024. [Manufacturer brochure](https://superflon.com/default/img/hblock/content/style98/pdf/SamIlFlonTech-ENG.pdf), PDF page 4, printed pages 6–7.

The phase entries do not name the supplied products or directly state a Pyeongtaek location. Project identity, scope and installed products require further matching. History years are not contract periods or commissioning dates. The separate 2024 UPW-tank entry names Samsung Engineering but no site; it is not assigned to P4.

PDF pages 10–11 explain general rotational lining and distinguish coating and lining production. These factory descriptions do not prove the manufacturing route or accepted tests of a particular delivery. Catalog performance and certification claims are not treated as verified site specifications. No material grade, lining thickness, duct size, quantity, route or building height is assigned.
