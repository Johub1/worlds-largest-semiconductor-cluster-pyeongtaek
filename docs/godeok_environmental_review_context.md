# Godeok public wastewater treatment: environmental-review context

Living project, updated continuously.

Reviewed source context, 16 November 2026. This adds context to the existing PT-I001 review item, rather than creating a new allegation.

## A documented expansion proposal

The eleventh environmental-preservation review opinion for project **HG2008B004**, the Pyeongtaek Godeok Internationalization Planning District General Industrial Complex, explicitly describes a proposed wastewater treatment capacity increase from **340,000 to 450,000 m³/day**, an increase of 110,000 m³/day (page 1). The EIASS progress list records receipt on 9 March 2023 and completion on 21 June 2023. These are consultation-process dates, not demonstrated commissioning dates or a separate wastewater basic-plan approval date.

The opinion describes combined treatment at the Godeok public wastewater treatment facility of sewage and wastewater from the industrial complex and wastewater from industrial facilities at the nearby Pyeongtaek Eco Center (평택 에코센터). This supports a conceptual explanation of the public treatment system's planned catchment. It does not identify internal Green-dong buildings or actual connecting pipe coordinates.

The document connects the 340,000 and 450,000 planning figures. The Han River Basin Environmental Office's 2026 integrated notice (No. 2026-15) still lists 340,000 m³/day across four stages; this review does not show whether or when the basic plan was amended. It does not, by itself, establish a separate basic-plan amendment, legal supersession, completed capacity or current throughput. The industrial-complex support schedule in Gyeonggi Notice 2023-181 separately records **345,000 to 450,000 m³/day**. The two earlier figures remain as stated in their respective documents; neither is silently corrected to match the other.

## Other reviewed opinions

The tenth and twelfth opinions, each two pages, concern park or green-area changes and associated mitigation obligations. They refer to public wastewater treatment and filtered nonpoint-pollution controls, but do not supply new fab heights, internal layouts or fab-specific treatment capacities. They do not establish where the four Green-dong buildings reported in a separate Samsung article are located.

## Sewage and industrial wastewater statistics have different scopes

The sewage plant and the industrial-complex wastewater facility appear in different official statistical categories with different stated locations. Whether they share a site or treatment trains is not established here.

The [2025 Gyeonggi statistical yearbook](https://ebook.gg.go.kr/src/viewer/main.php?category=1&host=main&page=584&site=20260608_143447), electronic page 584 / printed page 572, lists the Godeok public **sewage** treatment plant at Jwagyo-ri with a capacity of **108,000 m³/day**, in its public-sewage-facility table, whose yearly totals run through 2024; a separate reference date is not stated for each facility row. Do not equate this row with the industrial complex's public **wastewater** treatment record represented by `PUB-WWTP` and related conceptual elements.

The operating-statistics source and interpretation are documented in `meta.wwtp_operations_2024` of `data/campus.json`; the model's building-A note points to the facility-level record without distributing capacity to individual buildings.

The existing gas-supply utility description cites reporting published on [9 October 2026 (Yonhap)](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261008135600061) and [12 October 2026 (SBS)](https://news.sbs.co.kr/news/endPage.do?news_id=N1008792606) about planned reclaimed-water supply from the Godeok public sewage treatment plant. Both reports explicitly use the Korean sewage terms `공공하수처리장` and `하수처리수`; they do not identify the industrial wastewater plant as the source. This is a reported future reuse project, not evidence of completed supply, an internal fab pipe route, or a surveyed source-to-destination alignment. The yearbook's Jwagyo-ri row gives the sewage-facility category and capacity; the report itself does not give the source plant's street address. The press-reported reuse is not assigned a capacity share or a connection point in this model.
