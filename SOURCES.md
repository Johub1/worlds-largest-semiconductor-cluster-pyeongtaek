## Why Korean press remains in the list

Contract amounts, phase names, the substation, water supply volumes and local permits are reported in detail only by Korean outlets and Korean regulatory filings. International outlets such as Reuters, TrendForce and DIGITIMES cover the fab-level facts (which line makes what, investment decisions, schedules) and frequently cite those same Korean reports. Where both exist, the international source is listed first and the original report is kept so that the claim can be traced.
